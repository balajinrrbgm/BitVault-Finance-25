# 🚀 BitVault Finance - User Guide

## Quick Start

### 1. Connect Your Wallet
- Click "Connect Wallet" in the top-right corner
- Select your Starknet wallet (ArgentX or Braavos)
- Approve the connection

### 2. Navigate the Platform

#### 📍 Vaults
- Click "Vaults" to scroll to your vault dashboard
- View all your active vaults
- See balances, APY, and performance

#### 🌉 Bridge
- Click "Bridge" to open the Bitcoin bridge
- Deposit BTC from Bitcoin network to Starknet
- Withdraw BTC from Starknet to Bitcoin network

#### 📈 Yield
- Click "Yield" to explore yield strategies
- Choose from 4 different strategies
- Stake your BTC to earn passive income

#### 🔒 Privacy
- Click "Privacy" to access the privacy mixer
- Deposit BTC anonymously
- Withdraw to any address while preserving privacy

## Features in Detail

### 🌉 Bitcoin Bridge

**Deposit to Starknet:**
1. Select "Deposit to Starknet"
2. Enter the amount of BTC
3. Enter your Bitcoin address
4. Review fees (Network + Bridge)
5. Click "Deposit BTC"
6. Wait ~30 minutes for confirmation

**Withdraw to Bitcoin:**
1. Select "Withdraw to Bitcoin"
2. Enter the amount of BTC
3. Enter recipient Bitcoin address
4. Review fees
5. Click "Withdraw BTC"
6. Wrapped tokens burned, BTC sent

### 🔒 Privacy Mixer

**Step 1: Deposit**
1. Click "Privacy" → "Deposit (Private)"
2. Enter amount (0.01 - 10 BTC)
3. Click "Deposit Privately"
4. **SAVE YOUR SECRET NOTE!** (Can't be recovered)
5. Wait at least 30 minutes

**Step 2: Withdraw**
1. Click "Privacy" → "Withdraw"
2. Enter amount
3. Enter recipient Starknet address
4. Paste your secret note
5. Click "Withdraw Anonymously"
6. Funds sent privately!

**Security Tips:**
- ✅ Save secret note in password manager
- ✅ Wait 30+ minutes between deposit/withdraw
- ✅ Use different recipient addresses
- ✅ Never share your secret note

### 💼 Vault Management

**From Vault Cards:**
1. Find your vault in "My Vaults"
2. Click "Manage"

**Deposit to Vault:**
1. Select "Deposit" tab
2. Enter amount
3. See yearly/daily returns estimate
4. Click "Deposit to Vault"

**Withdraw from Vault:**
1. Select "Withdraw" tab
2. Enter amount (or click MAX)
3. Review new balance
4. Click "Withdraw from Vault"

**View Performance:**
- Current balance in BTC & USD
- APY percentage
- 24h performance
- Risk level
- Status (Active/Inactive)

## Transaction Flow

### All Transactions Follow This Pattern:

1. **Input** - Enter amount and details
2. **Validation** - Check wallet connection and balances
3. **Preview** - Review transaction details and fees
4. **Submit** - Click confirm button
5. **Pending** - See loading spinner (⏳ Processing...)
6. **Success** - See confirmation (✅ Transaction Successful!)

→ Press ESC key or click outside the modal

## APY Calculations

**Yearly Returns:**
```
Returns = Balance × (APY / 100)
```

**Daily Returns:**
```
Daily = Yearly Returns / 365
```

**Example:**
- Balance: 1 BTC
- APY: 12.7%
- Yearly: 0.127 BTC ($5,715)
- Daily: 0.000348 BTC ($15.66)
